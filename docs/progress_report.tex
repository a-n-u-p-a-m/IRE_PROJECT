Which stage of a two-stage cascade is responsible for its output quality? For a
\emph{pointwise} reranker, once the candidate set $C_q$ is fixed the final
ranking is invariant to the Stage-1 scores: Stage~1's only causal channel is
\emph{membership} in $C_q$ --- except under a budget, where Stage-1 rank decides
who survives the cut. Score-level attribution therefore cannot answer the
question, so we build four counterfactual worlds over a frozen universe $\Uq$
(actual, no-rerank, no-gate, budget) and read three non-additive estimands off
them. Their sign rules hold only \emph{conditionally on membership}, so all
validation is stratified and no pooled statistic is reported.

\textbf{Weeks 1 and 2 are complete}, with all three workstreams merged. No real
Stage-2 scores exist yet; that is Week~3.

\section{Key results}

\textbf{Feasibility gate passed.} Candidate Jaccard $0.11$--$0.16$, so the two
Stage-1 systems select genuinely different documents and the cross-paradigm
comparison has leverage. The expected confound --- TREC pools favouring lexical
runs --- does \emph{not} exist: judged density of $C_q$ is $1.06\times$ between
systems, and the Stage-1 floor is symmetric at an identical $0.450$ for both.
Outsider-policy supply is $40$--$49\times$ the budget, so \texttt{boundary} and
\texttt{alternative} are real policies rather than tie-breaks in disguise.

\textbf{Cost is not a constraint.} The cache keys on
$(\text{query},\text{doc})$ and every world re-ranks a subset of $\Uq$, so the
whole project costs $872{,}455$ calls \emph{once} --- a measured 27-minute job
at 535 pairs/sec. This retired the reference-subset sampling design.
Shuffle-invariance passed on the real reranker, verifying rather than assuming
the pointwise property the $E_{\text{gate}}$ derivation rests on.

\textbf{H7 is confirmatory for one policy only.} At $\Kc{=}100$ and the real
$|\Uq|$, no subset smaller than all 97 queries reaches $0.80$ power, and even
then only \texttt{alternative} clears it ($0.935$/$0.875$); \texttt{boundary}
falls short ($0.67$/$0.41$). Declared descriptive in advance.

\section{Corrections --- the fortnight's most valuable output}

Six defects were found and fixed, \textbf{four of them in checks that passed
while being structurally unable to detect the failure they existed to catch}: the H7 power denominator conditioned on testability and so overstated
power; ``relevant'' counted grade-1 documents that carry zero gain, inflating
the gate-failure supply $4$--$9\times$ and nearly deciding the headline $\Kc$
from the wrong number; the BM25 verifier used exponential gain where \texttt{trec\_eval} uses linear,
so it would have ``verified'' against an unreachable number; and the allocation
spend check compared against the amount \emph{achieved}, so a short allocation
matched its own shortfall.
Separately, our own pre-declared headline-$\Kc$ rule conflated a non-degeneracy
floor with a selection rule and so chose the depth with \emph{least} signal; the
correction is recorded as post-hoc, defensible only because supply is measurable
before any effect exists. The sixth was feature leakage: Stage-2 features over
all of $\Uq$ do not \emph{approximate} $e_{\text{gate}}$ for a non-survivor,
they \emph{determine} it, since a cached score outside $C_q$ exists only
because world~C was computed. Now restricted to survivors, with the parameter
renamed so the signature enforces it.

\section{Open validity threat, and how it is handled}

$\Uq$ is $1.65\%$ judged while BM25's top-100 is $51\%$ judged. World~C sorts
${\sim}9{,}000$ mostly-unjudged documents, so judged survivors are displaced out
of its top-10 and $E_{\text{gate}}$ drifts towards
$\mathrm{DCG}(\pi_{\text{act}})$ as an artefact of 2019 pooling. Resolved \emph{procedurally} at freeze~v2: every effect is computed both
uncondensed and condensed (Sakai, SIGIR 2007). Condensing transforms rankings,
not scores, so it costs no extra GPU calls, and it is the only option that does
not decide before the evidence exists. The uncondensed estimand stays primary;
a disagreement is itself a finding.

\section{Future work}

\textbf{Week 3.} Nothing is blocked. Regenerate the synthetic fidelity numbers
under the corrected feature boundary; fill the cache; compute $E_{\text{rerank}}$, $E_{\text{gate}}$ and
$\Delta_t$ over the real universe in both modes; and take the decisive
measurement --- if world C's top-10 is ${\sim}20\%$ unjudged the concern is a
footnote, if ${\sim}95\%$ the condensed figure becomes the headline.
\textbf{Week 4.} Effect distributions by stratum, the cost--fidelity curve
(which must converge to the exact reference at full budget), the H7 comparison,
predictor fidelity against all three baselines, and the reproducibility pack.

\textbf{Known risks.} Judgment sparsity is mitigated procedurally, not solved;
H7 is descriptive for \texttt{boundary} at any feasible size; and gate-failure
supply is thin even at the headline depth (median 6, with 17--23 of 97 queries
having none), so per-query distributions will be noisy and must be reported
with their supply.
